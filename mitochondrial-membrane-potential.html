Membrane Potential

TMRE is a dye that accumulates in mitochondria in proportion to their membrane potential — brighter signal means a healthier, more polarized mitochondrion. These models overlay TMRE signal onto mitochondrial shape across cisplatin dose and recovery timepoint, so you can see how potential drops and recovers alongside structure.
